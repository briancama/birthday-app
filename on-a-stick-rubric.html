Scoring

Every judge rates every stick 1–5 in four categories. The categories are weighted behind the scenes — here's the math.

Taste

×11 · 55 pts max
  • 5 = AMAZING — would eat a second stick immediately
  • 4 = Very Good — really enjoyable, well-balanced
  • 3 = Good — solid bite, nothing wrong
  • 2 = Not Great — some issues but edible
  • 1 = Poor — hard to finish

Presentation

×3 · 15 pts max
  • 5 = WOW FACTOR — you took a picture to show it off to strangers
  • 4 = Impressive — multiple elements, thoughtful styling
  • 3 = Nice — well-presented, attractive
  • 2 = Some Effort — at least it's arranged with intent
  • 1 = Basic — food. Stick. Nothing more.

Craft

×3 · 15 pts max
  • 5 = PERFECT — homemade components, real technique, serious effort
  • 4 = Skilled — techniques beyond basic assembly
  • 3 = Good — competent prep with thoughtful touches
  • 2 = Needs Work — mostly straightforward assembly
  • 1 = Poor — little more than plugging a stick into a pre-made component, minimal effort

Stickiness

×3 · 15 pts max

In 1929, the inventor of stick food patented the idea of “a food product on a stick for a handle.” In this challenge we hope to live up to his vision. Our last category is a bit nebulous but still crucial; it evaluates how well the stick functions as an integral part of the dish.

  • The stick is the handle — no plate, no fork, no second hand needed
  • The stick holds — it keeps everything together from the first bite to the last.
  • No stick, no dish — take the stick away and it just wouldn't be the same

Use your best judgment — score 1 to 5 on how well the entry lives up to the essence of the stick.

The Math

Total score: (Taste × 11) + (Presentation × 3) + (Craft × 3) + (Stickiness × 3) = up to 100 points

Winners: We will then add up each entrant's total score to determine the final placements with the highest score taking the top spot.

Example: 4 (Taste) × 11 + 3 (Presentation) × 3 + 3 (Craft) × 3 + 3 (Stickiness) × 3 = 77 points

House Rules

One entry per person. Bring enough for every judge to taste. You cannot score your own stick. Be sure to register by the competition deadline so everyone knows how many they'll need to produce.
