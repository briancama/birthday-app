Rules

  • Is it food?
  • Is it on a stick?

Each entry will be judged based on the following categories:

  1. Taste

    The most important category, make sure your entry tastes great.

  2. Presentation

    The more appetizing it looks the better. Garnishes, plating, and decoration all belong here.

  3. Craft

    Let everyone know that stick food can be gourmet. Show off the technique and effort that went into your dish to earn points in this category.

  4. Stickiness

    Look, we aren't the stick police, but we expect your food to be edible on a stick. If not, well, you'll lose some points here.
