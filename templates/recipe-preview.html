Championship Green Chili

Submitted by Marcus T. · Mains

The one that took home gold at the Chili Throwdown. Roasted Hatch chiles, pork shoulder, tomatillos, and a secret spice blend Marcus refuses to fully disclose.

Prep 25 min
Cook 2 hrs
Total 2 hrs 25 min
Serves 6–8
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork shoulder, cut into ½-inch cubes
  • 1 lb Hatch green chiles, roasted & peeled (or 2 × 7 oz cans)
  • 1 lb tomatillos, husked and quartered
  • 1 large white onion, diced
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • Fresh cilantro and lime wedges to serve

Instructions

  1. Season the pork cubes generously with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over high heat and brown the pork in batches until deep golden. Remove and set aside.
  2. Reduce heat to medium and cook the onion in the same pot until softened and translucent, about 6 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  3. Add tomatillos and cook until they begin to break down, about 5 minutes. Stir in cumin and oregano.
  4. Return the pork to the pot with the roasted chiles and chicken stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a gentle simmer.
  5. Cook uncovered for 1 hour 45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pork is tender and the sauce has thickened. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  6. Serve in bowls topped with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime. Goes great over rice or with warm flour tortillas.

Notes & Tips

Marcus's tip: Use fresh Hatch chiles when in season (August–September) and roast them yourself over a gas flame for the best flavor. Frozen roasted chiles are the next best option. The stew is even better the next day once the flavors meld.
